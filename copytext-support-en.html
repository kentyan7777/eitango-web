copytext Support & FAQ

Welcome to the official support page for the Safari extension "copytext". Here you can find setup instructions, frequently asked questions, and our contact information.

Getting Started (Setup Instructions)

■ On iPhone / iPad:

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Safari > Extensions.
  3. Find copytext and toggle it ON.
  4. Ensure website permissions are set to "Allow" so the extension can extract text.

■ On Mac:

  1. Open Safari.
  2. Go to Safari in the top menu > Settings (or Preferences) > Extensions tab.
  3. Check the box next to copytext to enable it.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. Is my browsing history or extracted text saved on any server?
A. No. All text processing and formatting are executed entirely on your local device. We never collect or transmit any of your personal data or page content.

Q. How much does the app cost? Are there any subscriptions?
A. copytext is available as a one-time purchase on the App Store. There are no monthly fees, hidden subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Once purchased, you have unlimited lifetime access to all features.

Q. Can I use the app across both Mac and iPhone?
A. Yes! copytext supports Apple Universal Purchase. A single purchase grants you full access on iOS, iPadOS, and macOS with the same Apple Account.

Q. The toolbar icon isn't extracting text on a specific website.
A. Some web pages using dynamic canvas layouts, PDFs, or complex iframes may prevent standard text extraction. Please ensure permissions are enabled for that website in Safari's extension settings.
