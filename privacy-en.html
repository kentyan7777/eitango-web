Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Kenichi Kawaguchi ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the iOS mobile application "My Dictionary" (the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect and Purpose of Use

We adhere to a strict data-minimization principle. We do not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or precise location).

Data processed within the App is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Vocabulary & Study History (On-Device Storage): All English words, meanings, notes, tags, sprint learning records, and study statistics entered or saved by you are stored locally on your device using Apple's local framework (SwiftData / Core Data). This data is never sent to, stored on, or inspected by our servers.
  • iCloud Synchronization (Apple Private Cloud): If you enable iCloud sync, your vocabulary library is securely synchronized across your own Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit infrastructure. This data is stored in your private iCloud container protected by your Apple ID, and we have no access to it.
  • Generative AI Linguistic Analysis & Audio Synthesis: To provide definitions, etymology, core images, grammatical breakdowns, contextual examples, and high-quality voice audio, the App transmits the text of the word you look up to our secure proxy server, which interacts with Google Gemini API and Google Cloud Text-to-Speech API. This query contains only the requested vocabulary word and necessary generation parameters. No personal identifiers or profiling data are associated with these requests.

2. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes reputable third-party services to deliver core functionality. Each service handles data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Gemini API & Google Cloud Text-to-Speech API Purpose: Linguistic analysis, learner-friendly definitions, contextual sentences, and synthetic speech generation.
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit) Purpose: Payment and subscription processing for My Dictionary Pro.
    All payment transactions are handled directly and securely by Apple Inc. We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers or billing details.
  • Apple CloudKit / iCloud Purpose: Optional multi-device synchronization within your personal Apple account.

3. Data Backup and Export

The App provides a built-in feature that allows you to export your entire vocabulary library as an encrypted JSON file at any time. The safeguarding and management of exported files remain the user's sole responsibility. We assume no liability for the loss, leakage, or misplacement of files once exported from the App.

4. Analytics and Crash Reports

The App does not integrate third-party tracking SDKs or advertising frameworks. We may receive anonymous, aggregated crash reports and basic engagement statistics provided automatically by Apple through App Store Connect, only if you have opted in to share diagnostic data with app developers in your iOS Settings. This information is used exclusively to improve app stability and fix bugs.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The App is suitable for general audiences and language learners of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data through the App, please contact us immediately.

6. Disclaimer

While our AI models strive to provide accurate, natural, and pedagogically sound English definitions and examples, we do not warrant the absolute accuracy, completeness, or suitability of AI-generated content. We shall not be held liable for any damages or inconveniences arising from the use of the App.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory updates. Any changes will become effective immediately upon posting the revised policy on this webpage. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
