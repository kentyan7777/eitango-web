Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Kenichi Kawaguchi ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information and data in the Safari Web Extension and application "copytext" ("the App").

1. No Data Collection and No External Transmission

The App is designed with privacy as a fundamental principle.

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, IP address, etc.). Furthermore, text extracted, viewed, or copied from web pages, as well as your browsing history, are never transmitted to external servers, saved remotely, or shared with third parties.

All text extraction, formatting, and operations are performed strictly and entirely on your local device.

2. On-Device (Local) Storage

User preferences such as theme selection (Light / Dark / Sepia), font styles, font sizes, and line number preferences are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage for your convenience. This data never leaves your device and is not synchronized across external networks.

3. Permissions

Permissions requested by the extension (such as active tab access) are used solely to extract the main article text from the active webpage and display it in the dedicated editor tab upon user interaction. Permissions are never used for background tracking or data gathering.

4. Apple Platform and Services

The download, purchase, and distribution of the App are handled by Apple via the App Store. Such transactions and platform data are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA: Apple Standard EULA).

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
