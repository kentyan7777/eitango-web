Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q

What is included in the Free tier vs. the Pro tier?

A

Free Plan: Includes up to 3 AI deep lookups per day, high-speed Sprint spaced repetition, and full offline access with no expiration date.

Pro Plan ($3.99/mo with a 7-day free trial): Unlocks unlimited AI deep lookups & 5-sentence-pattern grammar breakdowns, daily personalized AI Morning English Radio discussions crafted from your study words, unlimited vocabulary library capacity, and multi-device private iCloud automatic sync.

Q

Why is there no sound / audio pronunciation?

A

Please ensure that your iPhone is not in Silent Mode (check the physical Ring/Silent switch on the side or the Action Button settings) and that media playback volume is turned up. The app respects iOS audio session rules and also plays seamlessly through AirPods and Bluetooth headphones.

Q

Can I transfer my data when upgrading or switching to a new iPhone?

A

Yes! With Pro, your library automatically syncs across all your Apple devices (iPhone & iPad) via Apple CloudKit just by signing in with the same Apple ID. If you are using the Free tier, you can easily backup and restore your library anytime via Settings > Backup & Restore (JSON file export/import).

Q

How is My Dictionary different from other dictionaries or flashcard apps?

A

Traditional dictionaries offer static, often overly complicated definitions, while flashcard apps force you into rote memorization. My Dictionary is a "living electronic dictionary": you input only words you actually encounter in real life. Generative AI instantly provides learner-friendly definitions (based on Longman/LDOCE 2000-word defining vocabulary), an intuitive Core Image, nuance comparisons between tricky synonyms, and situational examples across Daily, Business, and Academic scenarios.

Q

Does the app work offline?

A

Yes. Once a word has been looked up or imported, its definitions, grammar notes, and audio pronunciations are cached locally on your device. You can study with Sprint mode and listen to continuous audio playback even on subways or flights without internet. When flying, activating Airplane Mode connects with your iPhone's barometric altimeter to launch a specialized high-altitude study experience.

Q

How do I cancel or manage my Pro subscription?

A

Subscriptions are securely managed by Apple. To manage or cancel, open the Settings app on your iPhone > tap your Apple ID / Profile at the top > Subscriptions > tap My Dictionary Pro. You can cancel anytime at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

Q

Power-User Tip: How do I set up iPhone "Back Tap" shortcuts?

A

You can look up or add words instantly from anywhere in iOS using iPhone's Back Tap feature:

1. Double-Tap Back: Quick Lookup of Copied Word

  1. Open Apple's Shortcuts app and tap "+" in the top right.
  2. Rename the shortcut to Quick My Dictionary.
  3. Add the action Get Clipboard.
  4. Add the action Search Word in My Dictionary and set its parameter to Clipboard.
  5. Save the shortcut.

2. Triple-Tap Back: Instant Add Copied Word to Library

  1. In the Shortcuts app, tap "+" and name it Add to My Dictionary.
  2. Add Get Clipboard.
  3. Add Add Word to My Dictionary and set its parameter to Clipboard.
  4. Save the shortcut.

3. Assign to iPhone Back Tap

  1. Open iPhone Settings > Accessibility > Touch.
  2. Scroll down to the bottom and tap Back Tap.
  3. Select Double Tap > choose Quick My Dictionary.
  4. Select Triple Tap > choose Add to My Dictionary.
